## 1. What this is

wamshare hosts `.wam` files — source text for the WAM model compiler — and
gives each one a link. When you open a link, **your browser** downloads the
source and compiles it. We store text and serve it back; we do not render your
model on our servers and we do not store the result.

The service is free, provided as-is, and run by one person as a side project.
Please size your expectations accordingly.

## 2. No accounts, only secrets

There is no sign-up. Your first upload returns a **secret**, and possession of
that secret is the only thing that proves an upload is yours. You may hold as
many secrets as you like; we cannot tell that two of them belong to the same
person, and we make no attempt to find out.

**If you lose a secret, it is gone.** There is no reset, no recovery and no
support route, because we hold nothing that could identify you. Anything
uploaded with that secret stays up and you can no longer delete it.

**Anyone holding your secret can delete everything behind it.** Treat it the
way you would treat a password.

## 3. Private and public

**Private** is the default. The model is unlisted, reachable only by its link,
and you keep all rights in it. We will not publish it, index it, or show it in
a gallery.

Private is *unlisted*, not *secure*. Anyone with the link can open it, and
anyone who happens to have the identical source file can find the same link.
Do not upload anything whose disclosure would harm you.

**Public** lists the model and dedicates it to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Anyone may use
it for anything, commercially included, with no obligation to credit you. We
cannot offer any other licence: every Creative Commons *licence* requires
attribution, and this service has no identity to attribute anything to.

**Publishing cannot be undone.** You can remove a model from the site later,
but a CC0 dedication applies permanently to copies already made. We can stop
distributing it. We cannot un-license it, and neither can you.

## 4. What you promise when you upload

By uploading you confirm that you own the source or otherwise have the right to
upload it, and that doing so breaks no law and infringes nobody's rights.

By publishing you additionally confirm that **you hold the rights you are
giving away**. A CC0 dedication is only effective if the person making it
actually owns the thing. We have no way to check this. If you dedicate
something that is not yours, that is on you.

Do not upload anything unlawful, anything that infringes someone else's rights,
or anything designed to harm the people who open it.

## 5. Nothing can be edited

Uploads are immutable — source, title and description are fixed when you upload
them. Fix a mistake by uploading a corrected version and deleting the first.

Identical source produces the same link, and **the first upload of a given
source wins**. If someone has already uploaded byte-for-byte what you are
uploading, you get their link, with their title, owned by their secret. You
cannot delete or publish it. Change anything at all and you get your own.

## 6. Deleting

With your secret you may delete one model or every model behind that secret.
Deleting removes the content; the link itself remains as a marker so that
anything derived from it does not break.

Deletion is not erasure of every trace. Others may already have copies,
especially of public models, and a CC0 dedication survives our deletion.

## 7. Lineage

An upload may declare that it derives from other public models. **These claims
are not verified.** We compare the two sources for textual similarity and show
what we measured; we do not police whether the claim is honest, and you should
not treat displayed lineage as authoritative.

Statistics shown with a model — triangle counts, checks and their results —
are reported by the uploading client and not independently verified, because
we never compile anything.

## 8. Availability

We may change, break, rate-limit or shut down the service at any time, without
notice, and delete anything on it.

Links are designed to be permanent. That is a design goal, not a promise. **Do
not rely on this service as your only copy of anything.** Keep your `.wam`
files.
